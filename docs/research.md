# Research

- [LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06120) — Reassess the current task instead of carrying forward early assumptions.

- [Intent Mismatch Causes LLMs to Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07338) — Identify the intent of the current request as the conversation changes.

- [Do LLMs Benefit From Their Own Words?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24287) — Use previous assistant replies for continuity without inheriting their errors or style.

- [Multi-User Chat Assistant (MUCA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04883) — Choose an appropriate conversational response with attention to participants and group context.

- [Humanlike Multi-user Agent (HUMA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17315) — Adapt conversational strategies and limit repetitive participation.

- [GroupMemBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14498) — Preserve who said what and distinguish participants' claims from established facts.

- [Evaluating Long-Horizon Memory for Multi-Party Collaborative Dialogues (EverMemBench)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01313) — Account for interleaved topics and information that changes over time.

- [Measuring Sycophancy of Language Models in Multi-turn Dialogues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23840) — Maintain factual correctness under conversational pressure.
